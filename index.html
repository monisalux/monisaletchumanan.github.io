About me

Hey! I'm Monisa — a Computer Software Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University (formerly Ryerson). I love working at the intersection of quality and engineering: making sure software not only ships, but ships right.

I've spent time as a QA Analyst at the Ontario Ministry of Health and currently work as a QA Specialist at EmergConnect, where I run functional, regression, and production testing across web and mobile platforms. I care a lot about making the testing process smarter — not just catching bugs, but helping teams understand why they happen.

Outside of work I enjoy building things from scratch — from AI-powered game agents to full-stack telecom apps. I'm always looking for ways to grow as an engineer.
